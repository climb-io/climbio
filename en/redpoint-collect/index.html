Redpoint Cat Collector

Save the crags you want to visit before you leave. When you get to the rock, you and your friends can pull up a topo even without a signal.

Download Chrome extension For desktop Chrome. Manual installation.

From your browser to the crag

  1. 01

    Install in Chrome

    Unzip the download. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked. Select the folder you unzipped.

  2. 02

    Save a crag

    Open a supported crag page and click 紅點貓收藏 in the Chrome toolbar. The extension opens Redpoint Cat on your computer and imports the routes and topos.

  3. 03

    Bring it on your phone

    Connect your computer and phone to the same Wi-Fi and open Redpoint Cat on both. Open My devices from the profile icon and pair them. Before leaving, open the crag on your phone to check that the photos and topos are there.

Which sites work with Collector?

Collector works with theCrag, Hong Kong Climbing, OpenClimbing, and sites that publish Climbio data.

Where are my saved crags stored?

Your saved crags go straight to your own Redpoint Cat library, with no sign-in. You can read them offline for personal use. Follow the source license before republishing any material.

How do I update the extension?

Download and unzip the new version. Replace the original folder, then find the extension at chrome://extensions and click Reload.
