How Hold Cat works

Photograph a wall, tap the holds, set a route.

Open Hold CatFull guide

Four steps

1

Photograph the wall

Start with a photo of your own wall.

2

Fix the holds

Holds are detected automatically; correct any that are off.

3

Set a route

Link holds into a route and view it right on the photo.

4

Save and share

Everything stays on your phone; it goes online only when you share.

Good to know

Are routes drawn onto the photo?

No. The photo stays a photo; routes and markers are stored separately, so you can change them any time.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Only signing in, sharing, and getting other people's data need a connection.

Many photos or a large project?

Use Wall Studio.
