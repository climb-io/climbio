How Red Dot Cat works

Keep the crag on your phone and read topos with no signal.

Open Red Dot CatFull guide

Four steps

1

Find or import a crag

Download from World, open a share link, or import a local data pack. You can also collect crags from supported sites on desktop.

2

Download to your phone

Crags, topo photos and routes are stored locally, so they work offline once downloaded.

3

Organize collections

Drag cards on the home screen to merge them. When sources share a parent, Auto-merge crags in Settings suggests candidates for you to confirm one by one.

4

Read topos, log climbs

Open a crag, pick a wall, tap a route for details, and log it.

Good to know

Whose data is it?

Author, URL and license stay with the original data. Saving a crag for yourself does not mean you may share it publicly. See Sources and licenses.

When does it update?

New crags must be imported manually. When a source you already saved has a new version, it is checked in the background as you open the crag, at a set interval per crag.

How is merging decided?

It looks for a common parent through the stable IDs in the source hierarchy, not just names or GPS. Merged items are not suggested again, the original data is kept, and you can split them any time.

Will it ask me to merge after an import?

This is still being wired up and verified. Until it ships, use manual batch merge in Settings.
