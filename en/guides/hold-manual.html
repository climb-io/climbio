Hold Cat guide

Photograph the wall, set your own routes.

Open Hold Cat

First time

  1. Photograph: tap +, take a photo or pick one from your library, and crop to the wall.
  2. Detect: wait for the holds to be found; long-press to add any that were missed while setting a route.
  3. Set a route: pick a grade, a start and a finish. Then you can view and share it.

Install

Hold Cat is a web app (PWA) that opens on phones, tablets and computers. No sign-in and no prior install are needed.

iPhone / iPad

Open Hold Cat in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

Open Hold Cat in Chrome and choose Install or Add to Home screen.

In-app browsers such as LINE may restrict file picking, storage and installing. Use Safari or Chrome instead.

Data is stored mainly in the app or browser on the current device. A different browser or another phone does not see the same data.

Add a wall

  1. Tap + on the home screen, then take a photo or pick one from your library.
  2. Crop so the wall you want to climb stays in frame.
  3. Tap Done and wait for detection to finish.
  4. Confirm the gym and wall name. You can change them later.

The phone uses the location in the photo to suggest nearby gyms. If there is no location, no permission, or no gym nearby, just pick one yourself.

Photos with clear holds, decent light and little glare detect best. For a very wide wall, use Studio to handle several photos.

Auto-detection

Hold Cat analyzes the cropped photo and outlines each hold, then goes straight to route setting.

  • Large volumes, shadows and occluded areas may be missed or misread; you can fix them by hand.
  • A first result appears quickly; refined outlines may need to load a model or go online.
  • If it fails, follow the prompt and retry. There is no need to retake the photo.

Loading the model for the first time, refined outlines and sharing need a connection.

Fix holds

In the route screen, long-press a hold: on a detected hold it opens editing, and on a missed spot it adds a hold.

  1. Long-press the hold to fix; the view zooms in nearby.
  2. Let the app re-trace the outline, or drag the control points of the outline yourself.
  3. Confirm, undo or delete with the floating tools.

When holds are close or overlapping, zoom in before selecting. Editing is limited while outlines are still processing in the background.

Set a route

  1. On the wall page, tap + or tap the wall photo.
  2. Pick a grade, then switch the type below: Start, Hand, Finish, Foot.
  3. Tap holds on the photo to add them to the route; tap again to change the type or remove.
  4. A start and a finish are required to complete the route. The name can come later.

A route can have at most two start holds. To note the move order, switch to Sequence and tap holds in order of movement; the same hold can be reused at different steps.

To change your own route, open it from the wall card or the route view. For a shared route you cannot edit directly, create your own version.

View a route

On the wall page, tap a route to open the full-screen route view.

Send log

In the route view, tap the check at the bottom right to mark a send; tap again to undo. It is only sent or not sent: no attempt count, date or flash.

The wall list can be filtered by sent or not sent, and sorted by grade or time. Logs are stored on this device; signing in does not sync them automatically.

Share

Once a route is done, you can share the route image or a link. Images suit group chats; to give someone a wall and routes they can work with, use the app's share or publish options.

  • Received a friend's link: check the source and contents first, then add it to your device.
  • Public links: share a supported route link with others; for a private handoff in person, use Nearby transfer instead.

Sharing a wall does not expose the whole phone's data. Before going public, check photo credit, the gym's consent and privacy.

Send to others (Nearby transfer)

  1. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi that permits device-to-device connections. Open Hold Cat and tap the nearby-transfer icon at the top right of Find walls.
  2. Select one or more nearby people and the walls to send, then tap Send to N people.
  3. Recipients tap Accept to receive the wall photo, holds and routes directly on their devices, or decline the request.

No pairing is required. A received wall stays local and is not automatically published. Your send history, settings and device keys are not shared with other climbers.

Guest Wi-Fi may block direct connections. QR-code connection is a fallback only when the discovery service is not configured or cannot be reached; it does not appear simply because nobody is nearby.

My devices (two-way sync)

  1. On both devices, open Avatar → Sync to other devices → Add new device.
  2. Choose the other device, compare the four-digit codes shown on both screens, and tap Confirm on each.
  3. After pairing, missing or newer walls, routes and send records sync automatically. Watch the Syncing and Synced statuses; there is no manual Send button.

Pair once, then reopen My devices on both devices while they can connect directly on the same network whenever you want to sync. Signing in or leaving the app in the background does not sync data.

Your own devices also exchange route-editing credentials. For 3D walls only the routes are synchronized; download models separately. Deletions do not sync and send records only accumulate. Use a local backup when direct connection is unavailable. Pair Hold Cat and Red Dot Cat separately.

Find walls

If the + menu on the home screen has Find walls, you can browse public gyms and walls; download a wall and it appears on the home screen. The list includes only walls with public data, and downloading needs a connection.

Backup and switching phones

Walls, routes, hold edits and send logs are stored mainly on the device. Back up your data as a local .climbio file or use My devices on the same network.

  1. On the old device, choose Back up data and save the .climbio file locally.
  2. On the new device, choose Restore data and select that .climbio backup file.
  3. Check that photos, routes and sends are correct before clearing the old device.

A regular browser tab and the home-screen install may use different storage. Do not clear site data before you have a backup.

Offline and updates

  • Works offline: saved photos and routes.
  • Needs a connection: loading the detection model the first time, some refined outlines, public sharing and receiving public walls.
  • Updates: the new version is fetched when online; back up before updating.

If a download or save fails, check the connection, permissions and device storage first.

3D and experimental features

The main flow is based on 2D photos; you do not need to wait for 3D. 3D reconstruction and detection research lives in Climbio Lab; bulk photo organizing and formal publishing use Studio. To shoot photos for 3D, see How to shoot 3D photos.

FAQ

Not all holds were found

Check the crop and sharpness first. Then, in the route screen, long-press a missed spot to add a hold, or long-press a misdetected hold to fix its outline.

Why does it download a model?

Detection and outline refinement may use different models, loaded on first use or when a model updates. Some refined features also need a connection.

Can I save a photo with no routes?

Yes. The wall is saved on the device first and you can set routes later. If detection fails, you do not need to retake the photo.

How do I edit my own route?

Open it from the route card on the wall page or the pencil icon in the route view. To adjust holds, long-press in the route screen.

My old walls are missing after switching phones

Data is stored locally. Pair and sync while both devices have My devices open on the same direct-connect network; otherwise restore a local .climbio backup. Check the new device before erasing the old one.

Can't find nearby gyms or public walls

Gym suggestions depend on location and permission, and the public list does not cover every gym. Pick a gym manually, or just photograph a wall to create your own.

Want to organize many photos, stitch walls or build 3D

Use Studio for bulk editing; see Lab for 3D and detection research.
