How Studio works

Turn photos into climbing data other people can read.

Four steps

1

Create a project

Start from photos or existing data.

2

Detect and edit

Indoors, Wall Studio handles walls, holds and routes. Outdoors, Crag Studio handles areas, topos and route info.

3

Link it together

Photos, walls, areas and routes are each connected, so every piece of information is maintained once.

4

Export and share

Export to the open Climbio format, readable by the readers and other tools. Check licensing before going public.

Good to know

What is the difference between the two studios?

Wall Studio is for indoor walls and Crag Studio is for natural crags. The data format is the same; only what you edit differs.

Is the data just images?

No. Photos and annotations are stored separately, and routes point to nodes and views, so other tools can read, display and keep editing them.

Is it open source?

The format and the Viewer and Editor components keep dependencies to a minimum. What is public is defined on GitHub.
