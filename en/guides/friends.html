Friends exchange

Pass a favorite wall or crag directly to someone you are climbing with. Once accepted, its photos and routes stay on their device, without going on a public website.

Climb together, keep your own copy

  1. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi that permits device-to-device connections. Open the same app on each device.
  2. Tap “+” on the home screen, choose “Send to friends”, then select nearby friends and what to send. You can send to several friends at once.
  3. Your friend taps “Accept” and receives the photos and routes. They can choose items to send back in another exchange.

Hold Cat exchanges with Hold Cat, and Redpoint Cat with Redpoint Cat. No pairing is required. Each send is one-way and does not automatically synchronize everything.

What can you exchange?

Hold Cat

Send a complete wall, including its photo, holds and routes. Friends can view the routes without making the wall public.

Open Hold CatUser guide

Redpoint Cat

Send a crag, photo topo or a personally saved crag or topo. Friends can keep the received content on their device for offline viewing.

Open Redpoint CatUser guide

An exchange is not a public publication and does not include climbing logs, personal settings or device keys. Saved third-party photos and routes remain subject to their original licenses. Check you have permission before sending them to others.

Friends exchange vs. My devices

Friends exchange: Pick the recipient and items. They must accept. There is no pairing or automatic sync.

My devices: Pair your own devices once. When both have their sync pages open and can connect, they exchange missing or newer personal data, including logs and saved crags.

If a nearby friend does not appear, check your Wi-Fi permits local connections. The app offers QR-code fallback only when the signaling service is unavailable.
