Shoot a batch of photos,get a 3D wall with holds marked.

No tracing holds by hand. The computer finds the holds in each photo, then lets the photos vote on where each hold sits on the 3D wall.

How it works

    Why vote

    One photo’s outline is a little off. Pasted straight onto the 3D wall, outlines spill over the surface. Photos err in different directions, so a vote cancels the errors and the outline hugs the hold.

    How to shoot

    Walk along the wall

    Turning on the spot gives no parallax, so depth cannot be computed. Shoot every small step, with about 70% overlap between neighbours.

    Left, middle, right

    Every spot needs three angles. Count left and right from 15° off straight-on.

    Lock the camera

    One lens throughout, no digital zoom. Long-press to lock focus and exposure, and avoid glare and backlight.

    Cover the awkward parts

    Corners, the top edge and caves get missed most. Review right after shooting and fill gaps on the spot.

    About 120 to 160 photos are enough. More just repeats.

    Can I film instead

    Not tested yet. Video gives a natural order and good parallax, but each frame has lower resolution than a photo, plus compression and stabilisation. Use 4K, turn stabilisation off, lock focus and exposure, walk slowly, then pull 2 to 3 sharp frames per second. Only a real test will tell.

    Limits

    Verified only on two photo sets of the Mingde wall. Very small holds, corners and caves are where it fails most.
