RouteKit

View and set routes on a wall photo

See the live demoView source on GitHub

What it is

Give it a wall photo and hold outlines and it draws the route image: route holds keep their color, everything else goes gray, with start, finish and move order marked. Users tap holds to build a route, and you can export a full-size route image.

What it does

Easy to read

Route holds stand out while the rest fades to gray; start, finish and foot tape are clearly marked.

Set routes with a tap

Tap to add a hold, change its role or remove it; start holds are capped and move order can be recorded.

Portable data

Reads and writes the Climbio public format and the circle format (RouteMake), with no tie to any app or backend.
