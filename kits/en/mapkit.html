MapKit

Vector crag maps you can view and edit

See the live demoView source on GitHub

What it is

Draws walls, trails, roads, areas, place names and rescue points as a clean vector map with automatic label avoidance, a scale bar and a compass. Toggle orthophoto and contour base maps, and let users edit wall lines and area outlines.

What it does

Clear, readable maps

Labels avoid each other, sector names appear as you zoom in, and pan, zoom and rotate feel natural on a phone.

Base maps on demand

OpenStreetMap, orthophoto and contours toggle independently; your own data still shows without a base map or network.

Editing that knows walls

Drag vertices, draw lines, flip which way a wall faces, undo/redo, and get a standard layer out.
