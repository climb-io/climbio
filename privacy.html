隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

Climbio（climbio.app，以下稱「本 App」）是抱石與戶外攀岩的路線工具（岩點貓、紅點貓與 Climbio Studio）。本 App 沒有帳號系統、不需要登入、沒有廣告，也不使用分析或追蹤工具。這份政策說明本 App 會處理哪些資料、存在哪裡。

1. 存在你裝置上的資料

  • 你拍的牆面照片、岩點、路線、牆面名稱，都存在你手機或電腦的瀏覽器裡（IndexedDB），不會存到我們的伺服器。
  • 語言、外觀、路線排序、是否看過導覽等偏好，存在瀏覽器的 localStorage。
  • 相機只用於拍照，畫面不會上傳。
  • 位置：用相機拍照新增牆面時，App 會向瀏覽器要一次目前位置（需要你同意）；從相簿選照片時，App 會在你的裝置上讀照片檔案裡記錄的拍攝位置（照片有記錄的話）。位置只在你的裝置上跟 App 內建的岩館清單比對，判斷這面牆在哪一間岩館（也用岩館名稱當牆面名稱）；存下來、同步的只有岩館名稱，分享路線時也會附上岩館名稱（讓大家找得到同一間岩館的牆）；位置本身不會上傳、不會存下來。不同意或判斷不到時，可以自己選岩館，也能照常使用。
  • 偵測岩點、描繪輪廓：新增牆面時，岩點偵測與描繪輪廓都在你的手機上執行，照片不會為了偵測離開手機（第一次會從本網站下載偵測模型）。
  • 清除瀏覽器資料或移除 App，這些資料會一併刪除，我們無法幫你復原。

2. 分享路線圖

  • 路線圖在你的裝置上產生。使用手機的系統分享或下載時，圖片本身不經過我們的伺服器。路線連結分享的是連結，不是圖片（見下一節）。

3. 公開分享、備份與裝置交換

  • 原則：你的資料預設只在自己的裝置上，沒有自動帳號同步，Climbio 不存你的照片、岩點、路線、攀爬紀錄。資料只在三種情況離開你的裝置：（1）你按「公開」的牆、岩場與 3D（放進 Climbio 的公開資料集，見下面「公開分享」）；（2）你手動按的備份（存成你自己保管的 .climbio 檔）；（3）附近交換與我的裝置（手機直接傳）。
  • 帳號：沒有帳號，也不用登入。公開的內容不記擁有者，任何人都能讀，也能發佈新版本；署名是你發佈時自己填的暱稱，會放在公開內容的署名裡。為了防止濫用，伺服器會依你的網路來源（只用雜湊判斷，不存 IP）限制每天的發佈次數。
  • 手機 id 與裝置金鑰：本 App 第一次打開時，在你的裝置上產生一組隨機的手機 id（不含姓名、email 或裝置資訊），用來認出這支手機出的線；在公開牆寫自己的線不用登入，App 會在你的裝置上產生一組簽章金鑰：私鑰只留在你的裝置（跟本機備份走），公鑰的指紋與你的線一起存在 Climbio，用來確認只有同一個人能修改、收回自己的線。這些不含姓名或 email。
  • 備份（手動）：「備份資料」產生的 .climbio 檔存在你選的地方，由你自己保管；備份不經過 Climbio 的伺服器，也不會上傳到任何雲端。
  • 附近交換與我的裝置：打開「附近交換」時，我們的伺服器會暫時記下你的裝置代號、自動取的名稱（例如「岩點貓1234」）、使用的 App 和對外 IP 的雜湊（用來找出同一個網路的人），約 12 秒沒有回應就當作離開、當天清除；連線訊息（WebRTC 的連線資訊）經過伺服器轉送後立刻刪除。用「掃 QR code」連線時完全不經過我們的伺服器。「我的裝置」配對過的裝置，伺服器另用「配對房間」（配對密鑰的雜湊前 24 碼）讓它們互相找到。你挑的牆面、岩場，以及配對裝置之間的攀爬紀錄與個人收藏，由手機直接傳給對方，不經過我們的伺服器。對方收下就是他手上的一份，我們無法收回。
  • 公開分享（岩點貓、Studio Wall 的公開牆；紅點貓、Studio Crag 的公開 Topo 與岩場；3D）：按「公開」的內容放進 Climbio 的公開資料集（Hugging Face 上的 climbio/walls、climbio/crags、climbio/walls3d、climbio/crags3d，由 Climbio 保管）；任何人不用登入就能讀、下載。牆的照片會去掉拍攝位置；岩場的照片若有拍攝位置（含朝向），為了在地圖上定位會一起公開。路線依你設定的簽名顯示。Climbio 的伺服器另外記索引：包編號、名稱、岩館或岩場、版本、時間、檔案名單和雜湊，讓「找牆面」與「世界」列出公開的內容；大家加的路線留在 Climbio 的資料庫（不含帳號），每天匯出到同一個資料集。授權為 CC BY-SA 4.0，見服務條款。
  • 公開後不能完全收回：公開資料集的檔案任何人都能下載，已經被下載的副本我們收不回。公開的內容沒有擁有者，Climbio 沒有「下架」：岩點貓的牆 90 天沒有更新會自動從清單拿掉，資料集的檔案保留給研究、不因要求刪除。只有侵害第三方權利、含可識別的個人資料、法院或主管機關要求時才會移除（要求請寫信到 climbio.service@gmail.com，附編號與理由）。要傳給特定的人，請用「附近交換」。
  • 訓練：沒有公開的資料（包括你的備份與本機照片）不會用來開發、改進或訓練任何 AI／機器學習模型。你按「公開」放進開放資料集的內容，依 CC BY-SA 4.0 授權，任何人（包括 Climbio）都可以用來訓練模型，Climbio 會用公開資料集和 Studio Wall 的修正記錄改進岩點偵測。

收到分享的人打開公開內容時，本 App 直接從公開資料集（Hugging Face 的 CDN）讀取，Hugging Face 會看到你的 IP 位址等連線資訊，內容存成收到的人自己的一份。公開的牆，App 會向 Climbio 的索引問這面牆有哪些人的路線。出線者改過再分享的是另一條新線，收到的人會看到新線提示，收下才加進來。

4. 瀏覽器擴充「紅點貓收藏」

  • 用途：在支援的岩場頁（theCrag、OpenClimbing、香港攀岩網站，或頁面放了 Climbio <link rel="climbio"> 的網站）按工具列的按鈕，擴充讀取該頁、下載 Topo 照片、轉成 Climbio 格式，再交給同一個瀏覽器裡的紅點貓分頁。
  • 全部在你的瀏覽器裡：讀取、下載、轉換都在你的電腦上做，Climbio 的伺服器收不到任何資料。沒有登入、沒有帳號、沒有分析或追蹤、沒有遠端程式碼。收好的岩場暫存在擴充自己的儲存空間（IndexedDB climbio-collect），紅點貓確認匯入後就刪除。
  • 權限：activeTab、scripting 只在你按下按鈕時讀取目前這一頁；storage 記錄要收藏哪個網站與紅點貓的網址；網站權限（image.thecrag.com、i0.wp.com、hongkongclimbing.com、api.openstreetmap.org、commons.wikimedia.org、upload.wikimedia.org、photos.app.goo.gl、photos.google.com、lh3.googleusercontent.com）只用來下載你要收藏的頁面上的照片與公開資料。只有 climbio.app、它的開發版與 localhost 的頁面可以連進擴充。
  • 其他網站看得到你的連線：擴充直接從那些網站下載，它們會看到你的 IP 位址等連線資訊，並依各自的政策處理。
  • 來源與授權：收藏保留原始來源網址與可取得的作者、授權資訊；只供你私人離線閱讀，不代表取得公開轉載的權利，紅點貓也不開放分享個人收藏。
  • 我們不販售、不出租任何資料，也不把資料用於廣告或與擴充單一用途無關的目的。

5. 第三方服務

本 App 使用下列服務，這些服務會依各自的隱私權政策處理你的連線資訊（例如 IP 位址）：

  • Firebase Hosting：提供本 App 的網頁與輪廓模型。
  • Google Firebase（Firestore、Cloud Functions、App Check）：公開分享的索引與防止濫用。見 Google 隱私權政策。
  • Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise（Firebase App Check）：確認請求來自真的 Climbio 網頁，防止濫用；會把裝置與瀏覽器的特徵送給 Google，並適用 Google 的隱私權政策與服務條款。
  • Hugging Face（huggingface.co）：託管公開資料集（climbio/walls、climbio/crags、climbio/walls3d、climbio/crags3d），公開的牆與岩場放在這裡，任何人不用登入就能讀；下載者的連線資訊（例如 IP 位址）由 Hugging Face 依其隱私權政策處理。
  • jsDelivr、Google 的 gstatic：載入程式庫（偵測引擎、Firebase）。
  • Google Fonts：載入路線圖上的字型（只下載字型檔，路線圖本身不會傳出去）。
  • 其他網站的路線連結：Climbio 也收別人架的站分享的路線。打開或掃描這種連結時，本 App 直接從那個網站下載路線、照片和縮圖，那個網站會看到你的 IP 位址等連線資訊。那些網站不是我們經營的，依它們自己的政策處理。

我們不販售、不出租任何資料，也不把資料用於廣告。

6. 你的選擇與權利

  • 不分享的時候，本 App 只為了確認請求來自真的 Climbio 網頁（App Check）連到 Firebase。
  • 你可以隨時刪除自己裝置上的牆面與岩場。公開分享的內容沒有擁有者，不能下架（見上面「公開後不能完全收回」）。
  • 有任何隱私問題，請寫信到 climbio.service@gmail.com 聯絡我們。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Climbio (climbio.app, “the App”) is a bouldering route tool. The App has no account system, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. This policy explains what data the App handles and where it is stored.

1. Data on your device

  • Your wall photos, holds, routes and wall names are stored in your browser on your phone or computer (IndexedDB). They are not stored on our servers.
  • Preferences such as language, appearance, route sort order and whether you have seen the intro are stored in the browser’s localStorage.
  • The camera is used only to take photos; the camera feed is not uploaded.
  • Location: when you take a photo to add a wall, the App asks the browser for your current location once (with your permission); when you pick a photo from your library, the App reads the capture location recorded in the photo file on your device (if the photo has one). The location is compared only on your device with the climbing gym list built into the App, to tell which gym the wall is in (and to name the wall after it). Only the gym name is stored and synced, and shared routes include the gym name (so others can find walls at the same gym); the location itself is not uploaded or stored. If you decline or no gym is found, you can choose the gym yourself, and the App works normally.
  • Hold detection and outline tracing: when you add a wall, hold detection and outline tracing both run on your phone, so the photo does not leave your phone for detection (the detection model is downloaded from this site the first time).
  • Clearing your browser data or removing the App deletes this data, and we cannot recover it.

2. Sharing a route picture

  • Route images are created on your device. System sharing and downloads do not upload the image to our servers. Route links share a link, not an image (see the next section).

3. Public sharing, backup and device exchange

  • Principle: your data stays on your device by default and there is no automatic account sync; Climbio does not store your photos, holds, routes or climbing log. Data leaves your device only in three cases: (1) walls, crags and 3D content you make public (placed in Climbio’s public datasets, see “Public sharing” below); (2) a backup you start by hand (saved as a .climbio file that you keep yourself); (3) Nearby exchange and My devices (phone to phone).
  • Account: there are no accounts and no sign-in. Public content has no owner: anyone can read it and publish a new version of it; the signature is the nickname you type when you publish and appears in the content’s credits. To prevent abuse the server limits daily publishing by your network origin (judged from a hash; the IP address is not stored).
  • Device ID and device key: the first time you open the App, it creates a random device ID on your device (no name, email or device information) to recognize routes set from this phone. To write your own routes on a public wall without signing in, the App creates a signing key on your device: the private key stays on your device (and travels with your local backup); the fingerprint of the public key is stored with your routes at Climbio so only the same person can edit or withdraw them. None of this includes a name or email.
  • Backup (manual): “Back up data” produces a .climbio file saved where you choose and kept by you; backups do not pass through Climbio’s servers and are not uploaded to any cloud.
  • Nearby exchange and My devices: when you open “Nearby exchange”, our server temporarily records your device code, an automatic name (for example “Hold Cat 1234”), the App in use and a hash of your public IP (to find people on the same network); after about 12 seconds without a response you are treated as gone, and the data is cleared that day; connection messages (WebRTC connection information) are deleted right after being relayed. “Scan QR code” connections do not pass through our servers at all. For devices paired under “My devices”, the server also uses a “pairing room” (the first 24 characters of a hash of the pairing secret) so they can find each other. The walls and crags you pick, and climbing logs and personal collections between paired devices, go directly phone to phone, not through our servers. Once received it is the receiver’s copy and we cannot take it back.
  • Public sharing (Hold Cat and Studio Wall public walls; Redpoint Cat and Studio Crag public topos and crags; 3D): what you make public goes into Climbio’s public datasets (climbio/walls, climbio/crags, climbio/walls3d and climbio/crags3d on Hugging Face, held by Climbio); anyone can read and download it without signing in. Wall photos have location removed; if a crag photo has a capture position (with heading), it is published too so it can be placed on the map. Routes show the signature you set. Climbio’s servers also keep an index: pack ID, name, gym or crag, version, time, file list and hashes, so “Find walls” and “World” can list public content; routes people add stay in Climbio’s database (without accounts) and are exported daily to the same dataset. The license is CC BY-SA 4.0, see the Terms of Service.
  • Public cannot be fully taken back: anyone can download the dataset files and we cannot recall copies already downloaded. Public content has no owner and Climbio has no “unpublish”: Hold Cat walls with no updates for 90 days are removed from the list automatically, and the dataset files are kept for research and are not deleted at anyone’s request. We only remove files that infringe third-party rights, contain identifiable personal data, or that a court or authority orders removed (write to climbio.service@gmail.com with the ID and the reason). To send something to specific people, use Nearby exchange.
  • Training: data you don’t make public (including your backups and local photos) is never used to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning model. Content you make public goes into the open datasets under CC BY-SA 4.0: anyone, including Climbio, may use it to train models, and Climbio uses the public datasets and Studio Wall corrections to improve hold detection.

When someone receives a share by opening public content, the App reads it directly from the public dataset (Hugging Face’s CDN), which sees your IP address and other connection information, and saves its own copy. For a public wall the App asks Climbio’s index which people have routes on that wall. If the setter edits and shares again, that is a new route; the receiver sees a new-route notice and adds it only by accepting.

4. Browser extension “Redpoint Cat Collect”

  • What it does: when you press the toolbar button on a supported crag page (theCrag, OpenClimbing, Hong Kong climbing sites, or a page with a Climbio <link rel="climbio">), the extension reads that page, downloads its Topo photos, converts them to the Climbio format and hands the result to Redpoint Cat in a tab of the same browser.
  • Everything stays in your browser: reading, downloading and converting happen on your computer. Climbio’s servers receive nothing. There is no sign-in, no account, no analytics and no remote code. The collected crag is held temporarily in the extension’s own storage (IndexedDB climbio-collect) and deleted once Redpoint Cat confirms the import.
  • Permissions: activeTab and scripting read only the page you are on, only when you press the button. storage remembers which site is being collected and the Redpoint Cat address. The site permissions (image.thecrag.com, i0.wp.com, hongkongclimbing.com, api.openstreetmap.org, commons.wikimedia.org, upload.wikimedia.org, photos.app.goo.gl, photos.google.com, lh3.googleusercontent.com) only let the extension download the photos and public data of the pages you collect. Only climbio.app, its dev site and localhost pages may talk to the extension.
  • Other sites see your connection: the extension downloads directly from those sites, which see your IP address and other connection information and follow their own policies.
  • Source and license: collected content keeps its source address and any available author and license information. It is for your private offline reading and does not grant public redistribution rights; Redpoint Cat does not let you share personal collections.
  • We do not sell or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising or for anything unrelated to the extension’s single purpose.

5. Third-party services

The App uses the following services, which handle your connection information (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Hosting: serves the App and the outline model.
  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check): the index of public shares and abuse prevention. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise (Firebase App Check): confirms requests come from the real Climbio pages to prevent abuse; it sends device and browser characteristics to Google and is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co): hosts the public datasets (climbio/walls, climbio/crags, climbio/walls3d, climbio/crags3d); public walls and crags live there and anyone can read them without signing in. Downloaders’ connection data (such as IP address) is handled by Hugging Face under its privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr and Google’s gstatic: load libraries (detection engine, Firebase).
  • Google Fonts: loads the fonts used on route images (only font files are downloaded; the image itself is not sent anywhere).
  • Route links from other sites: Climbio also accepts routes shared by sites other people host. When you open or scan such a link, the App downloads the route, photo and thumbnail directly from that site, which sees your IP address and other connection information. Those sites are not run by us and follow their own policies.

We do not sell or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Your choices and rights

  • If you don’t share, the App connects to Firebase only to check that requests come from the real Climbio page (App Check).
  • You can delete walls and crags on your own device at any time, Public content has no owner and cannot be unpublished (see “Public cannot be fully taken back” above).
  • For any privacy question, email us at climbio.service@gmail.com.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
